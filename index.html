02 — Why Excel, why automation

You already trust the spreadsheet. I make it dependable.

Most small businesses are not losing money because they lack software. They are losing money to the version of the file nobody understands, the stock figure that is three days old, and the Sunday evening spent rebuilding a report. A properly built workbook fixes all three.

  • One input, every view. Type a sale once; the dashboard, the stock sheet and the monthly report all know about it.
  • Protected structure. Nobody can accidentally delete a formula or type a date into a number column.
  • No subscription. It is your file, on your machine or your OneDrive. It keeps working if we never speak again.
  • Documented and handover-ready. A short guide, a commented formula layer and a walkthrough call.

03 — How a build runs

Four steps, no mystery.

Most catalog builds are delivered in 5–10 working days. Custom systems depend on how messy the input is — I will tell you honestly after I have seen your files.

STEP 01

Discovery call

A short call and a look at the spreadsheet you use today. We agree what the system must do and what it must not do.

STEP 02

Blueprint

You get a written structure: sheets, tables, rules and the exact deliverables. Nothing is built before you approve it.

STEP 03

Build & test

The workbook is built, protected, documented and stress-tested with your real data shapes and awkward edge cases.

STEP 04

Handover

A walkthrough call, a short manual, and 30 days of support for fixes and questions. The file is yours.

06 — Questions

Straight answers.

The five things people ask before they trust an Excel system with their business.

Read the full FAQ
Is this a real Excel file or an online app?
A real .xlsx workbook. It opens in Excel (Microsoft 365 or 2016+) and, where noted, in LibreOffice. You work in a file you own — nothing is hosted, nothing expires.
Do I need macros or a subscription?
No subscription, ever. Most systems run on formulas and Power Query, which need nothing extra. Where a macro genuinely saves time I add it and explain the security setting, but the workbook is designed to keep working without it.
Can it use the spreadsheet I already have?
Yes — that is usually the best starting point. I clean and restructure what you have instead of forcing you onto a new layout, so your product codes, customers and history stay intact.
How long does a build take?
Catalog systems: 5–10 working days. Custom builds: usually 2–4 weeks, depending on data cleanup and how many people need to use it.
What if I break something after handover?
Worst case, you restore a backup — the routine is part of the handover. Support is included (14–60 days depending on the package) and later fixes are quoted as small jobs.
