The build process

You always know what happens next.

No hourly mystery, no scope creep, no invoices that appear from nowhere. Every stage has a deliverable you approve before the next one begins.

01 — DISCOVERY

We look at what you use now

A 30–45 minute call plus your current file. I write down the workflow, the exceptions and the reporting you actually need — not the one a template assumes.

02 — BLUEPRINT

A written scope, a fixed price

Sheets, tables, rules, deliverables and a date. You approve the blueprint in writing; anything outside it is a separate, clearly priced change.

03 — BUILD

Built, protected, tested

You get progress previews. Testing uses your real data shapes — including the awkward cases: duplicate codes, refunds, half-empty rows.

04 — HANDOVER

Training and support

A walkthrough call (recorded if you like), a written manual, a backup routine and a support window for fixes.

What I will not do

  • Pretend a workbook is a certified accounting or e-invoicing system — it is neither.
  • Sell you a subscription for a file you can own.
  • Deliver something with no documentation, so you depend on me forever.
  • Promise integrations I have not verified against your actual tools.
