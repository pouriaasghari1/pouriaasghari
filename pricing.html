Logistics

How payment works.

When do I pay?
50% when you approve the written blueprint, 50% on handover. Custom builds over $1,500 can be split into three payments.
What payment methods?
Bank transfer (SEPA) or a payment link. An invoice is issued for every payment.
What if I need changes later?
Small adjustments are quoted as separate mini-jobs. Extended support ($65/month, ≈ €60) covers fixes and small changes without a new quote each time.
Is there a refund policy?
If the delivered workbook does not match the approved blueprint, I fix it. Beyond that, custom work is not refundable once the build starts — which is why the blueprint step exists.
Do you work with businesses outside the EU?
Yes. Prices are quoted in USD with a EUR reference; payment links handle most currencies.

Not sure which package you need?

Send a two-line description of the problem. I will tell you which package fits — and if the cheapest one genuinely works, that is the one I will quote.
