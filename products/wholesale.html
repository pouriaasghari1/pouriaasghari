Wholesale System B2B

Wholesale Order Hub

Purchase orders, trade accounts, backorders and receivables without a single lost email.

Best for: Wholesalers and B2B distributors Native .xlsx 5–10 working days
wholesale · dashboard mockup

Original dashboard mockup drawn for this catalog. Figures shown are sample data, not customer results.

What you get

Everything in the box.

A B2B order desk for trade customers: PO tracking, fill rates, credit exposure and delivery schedules in one workbook.

  • Trade account register with agreed price lists
  • Purchase order pipeline with status flow
  • Fill-rate and backorder tracking
  • Receivables ageing by customer
  • Delivery schedule and reminder sheet
Inside the workbook

A structure you can actually maintain.

tbl_PurchaseOrders

PO header plus lines, with a validated status flow so no order silently skips a stage.

tbl_Accounts

Trade customers, agreed discount levels, credit limits and payment terms.

Pipeline

Kanban-style status counts: confirmed, picking, in transit, delivered.

Receivables

Ageing buckets (current, 1–30, 31–60, 60+) calculated from invoice dates.

Delivery planner

One sheet showing what must leave the warehouse this week.

Why it holds up

Design decisions that matter at 6pm on a Friday.

Multi-line POs without chaos

One PO can hold dozens of lines with per-line quantities and prices, while the header stays a single order.

Fill rate that is calculated

Delivered lines ÷ ordered lines, per PO and per account — no manual percentage typing.

Credit visibility

Outstanding balances per account against agreed limits, so you see risk before you ship.

Status that cannot skip

Dropdown validation and conditional formatting keep the workflow honest.

Questions
Can several people work in it?
The workbook can sit on a shared drive or in OneDrive/SharePoint for multi-user editing. For heavier use we split entry and reporting into two files linked by Power Query.
Does it produce customer-facing documents?
Yes — a branded order confirmation and delivery note layout are included.
Can it link to my accounting app?
Through CSV import/export, usually. It is not a live two-way accounting integration.
