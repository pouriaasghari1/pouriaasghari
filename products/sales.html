Sales System

Sales Performance Studio

Every sale in one place, with revenue, margin and channel performance that update themselves.

Best for: Shops and distributors invoicing from Excel Native .xlsx 5–10 working days
sales · dashboard mockup

Original dashboard mockup drawn for this catalog. Figures shown are sample data, not customer results.

What you get

Everything in the box.

Excel sales system that turns order entry into revenue, margin and channel dashboards — no manual reporting.

  • Fast order entry with product and price lookup
  • Automatic revenue, margin and AOV calculation
  • Product, channel and customer breakdowns
  • Monthly target vs actual tracking
  • Returns and discount handling
Inside the workbook

A structure you can actually maintain.

tbl_Sales

Order lines with date, product, quantity, unit price, discount and channel.

tbl_Products

Product and price list the order form looks up, so prices cannot be mistyped.

Dashboard

Revenue vs target, channel mix, top products and daily averages.

Targets

Monthly targets per channel; the dashboard shows the gap and the run rate.

Reports

A printable monthly summary with the numbers your accountant asks for.

Why it holds up

Design decisions that matter at 6pm on a Friday.

Entry designed for speed

Typing a product code fills the name and price. Duplicate order numbers and impossible quantities are blocked on entry.

Margins you can trust

Cost sits on the product table, so gross margin is calculated per line and per product, not estimated.

Targets and run-rate

See whether the month is on track, not just what happened last month.

Clean hand-off

One click produces a monthly summary sheet and PDF for your accountant.

Questions
Can it import my till or POS exports?
Yes — CSV exports from most POS systems can be dropped into an import sheet that cleans and appends them automatically.
Does it handle VAT?
Yes. Prices can be entered gross or net and the workbook shows both, with the VAT rate on each line.
Can I invoice from it?
The system generates a clean invoice layout you can print or export to PDF. It is not a certified e-invoicing platform, but it produces the document.
